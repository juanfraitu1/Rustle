# SQANTI3 and the gffcompare scorecard on CAT/Liftoff v2.0 (CAT re-run step 4, 2026-10-01)

Step 4 of `docs/CAT_RERUN_PROTOCOL_2026-10-01.md`. Same six chromosomes, same transcript GTFs, same tool versions and flags as the
registered RefSeq runs (`bench/SQANTI3_POLISH.md`, `bench/ASSEMBLY_POLISH.md` "28 of 30 cells"); only the reference changes. Nothing
was re-tuned (R6). Registered outputs untouched: new runs live in `bakeoff/human_chrN/sq3_cat/` and `bakeoff/human_chrN/gffship_cat/`.

- Reference: `chrN_ref_cat.gtf` from `chm13v2.0_CAT_Liftoff.slim.gff3.gz` (`bench/annotation/cat_ref_gtf.py`; gene_id = CAT gene id,
  gene_name = symbol). Transcript counts equal the GFF's on every chromosome. CAT has 25% more reference transcripts than RefSeq on these
  six chromosomes (gffcompare reference mRNAs 57,605 vs 46,077).
- Arms: ours = `ship/SHIP.gtf` (the shipped polish setting), StringTie = `stringtie/st.gtf` (`-L -p 4`), FLAIR = `flair/flair.isoforms.gtf`.
- SQANTI3 5.5.4: `sqanti3_qc.py --report skip -t 4`, then `sqanti3_filter.py rules --skip_report` (`bench/annotation/sq3_cat_run.sh`).
  gffcompare 0.12.10, `-r chrN_ref{,_cat}.gtf` (`bench/annotation/gffcmp_cat_table.py`).
- **Reproduction check:** re-scoring the registered RefSeq runs with the new table scripts gives exactly the registered numbers
  (SQANTI3 pooled ours FSM 2,819 / PASS 5,705; gffcompare 28/30, pooled chains 2,809 vs 2,761).

**27/30 on CAT, but the misses move.** chr5, the RefSeq miss that 9 closing attempts could not fix, is 5/5 on CAT; chr14 becomes the
miss (2/5: chains 425 vs 427, intron-chain Sn 5.7 vs 5.8, transcript Sn 4.9 vs 5.0). As on RefSeq, every missing cell is a recall cell
lost by at most 2 chains / 0.1 point, and precision is ahead on all six chromosomes under both references. The pooled lead grows
(+83 chains vs +48; transcript precision 47.3 vs 45.4).

⚠ The registered statement "chr5 and chr11 have disjoint feasible regions" (row 863, `ASSEMBLY_POLISH.md`) is a statement about the
RefSeq reference: under CAT both chr5 and chr11 are 5/5 at the shipped setting. Which chromosome misses by 1-2 chains depends on the
annotation, so the 28/30 vs 30/30 frontier was reference noise at this margin, not a property of the assembler. No parameter was
re-searched on CAT (R6).

Sensitivities are lower on CAT for every tool (the denominator is 25% larger); precision is higher for ours and StringTie, lower for
FLAIR.
