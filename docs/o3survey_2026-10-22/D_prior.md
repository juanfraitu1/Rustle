# D. What this thesis already tried for O3 on RNA reads (inventory, detection power, dead ends, a screening score)

Audit written 2026-10-22, read-only. Sources read: the vault `Topics/O3.md`, about 25 `memory/project_o3_*`, `project_o2_*` and `feedback_o3_*` notes, `docs/archive/2026-10/merged/O2O3_EXPLORE_2026-10-22.md`, `docs/NEGATIVE_RESULTS_REGISTER.md`, and the frozen O2-VC outputs (descriptive counts only, no truth used).
Citation key: `rNNNN` = numbered register row; `L###` = line in the register for an older unnumbered row; `mem:` = memory note; `[unreg]` = result that is not in the register.

## 0. Bottom line
1. **Every RNA-only positive control was a DELETED copy (excision or masking).** The deleted copy's reads are absorbed by a paralog with `de` close to the copy's divergence d (r1089: 100% absorbed, d = 0.5-5%). Real absent copies are mostly near-identical (r1205: 114 of 127 haplotype-only loci lie within the allele cut-off delta = 0.958%), so they are alleles by construction. This is also PRAMEF7.1's regime.
2. **Detection has worked, but only where the copy's reads form their own cluster or carry linked private variants and sit more than about 1% from every present copy.** Examples: r1203 LR 8.2, r1363 two units, r1292/1293 sim 425/426. Placement and read recovery fail separately (r1363: 7 of 37 reads; r1333 FAIL).
3. **Copy versus allele cannot be settled on RNA alone** (L720: raw flag 7.39% of single-copy genes vs 7.93% background; L767). Any O3 output is a ranked "look harder" flag, as the brief says.
4. **The vault's `Topics/O3.md` lists no RNA-only SCREENING method with measured performance.** The closest is Tomaszkiewicz_2023 (bonobo BPY2 transcripts from a different genomic region, "from RNA alone", a curated call, not a screen). Its one method, devider, is REFUTED (6 of 6 clauses). The 10-22 additions are DNA-side: Paraphase counts haplotypes, Parascopy uses depth, DuploMap abstains at MAPQ 0 ("possibly from missing duplicated sequences in the reference").
5. **Two reporting traps.** O3 rates have been restated three times (37.14% -> 31.09% -> 25.44%, L781/L790; whole-genome held-out 10/37 at FPR 1/50, L952). Many "claims" are simulation-only (r1297-r1303).

## 4. Q3: a simple RNA-only screening score from the components that worked
**Name: Copy-Configuration Residual (CCR).** It is a ranked list per family, not a call. It is a rank over three binary signature classes, with no weights and no learned cut. It assembles components that already exist in the thesis; it claims no new principle.
| Item | Content |
|---|---|
| (a) signal | Per (family, copy or tied pair): **F** a linked foreign-allele cluster (at least 2 reads sharing at least 2 alleles no tied copy's genotype carries); **H** a copy with a `HomRef` VCF record at AF >= 0.10 (the caller saw the allele and wrote reference: two donor copies pooled on one reference copy); **P** (H5) a tied pair with at least 2 columns where ref_A = ref_B, VC separates them, exactly one copy has left its reference base, and neither has a 0/1 record (a reference copy the donor lacks, taught by its paralog). Score = (number of classes firing, number of independent columns or reads); order lexicographic, ties reported as sets. |
| (b) mechanism | F: a sequence no present copy explains (absent or donor-only copy; r1292). H: PRAMEF10 (reference .4's alleles pooled on .1). P: PRAMEF7.1 (28 teachers, four hom-alt private SNVs, no hets). |
| (c) literature | Topics/O3: DuploMap, "reads discordant at >= 5 reliable PSVs ... possibly from missing duplicated sequences in the reference"; Paraphase, "CN read from the number of phased haplotypes". The vault gives no RNA-only precedent (IsoCon: "does not allow us to conclusively assign each transcript to a gene copy", r1356). |
| (d) confounders | Alleles (het 0.1% flagged 4/8 intact families, r1304); allele-specific expression (the AF 0.2-0.8 band was set by eye); RNA editing (linkage test: 0/20 false flags in sim, r1293); gene conversion and mosaics (GOLGA8); chimeras (IG V(D)J, L716); PAR and X/Y gametologs (SLC25A6 gives 7 F flags); liftover and chain gaps (NPIPB2 57-bp insertion); weak-site caller instability (longcallR varied in 4/41 runs); other-family reads (r1295). |
| (e) thresholds | Combinatorial. Two constants, both pre-existing: AF >= 0.10 (the caller's own floor) and at least 2 columns/reads (set by eye in G3/H5). No weights. |
| (f) compute | F from `flags.tsv`; H from `vcf/*.vcf` (FILTER = HomRef, FORMAT AF); P from `columns.tsv` plus `vcf` genotypes (columns where ref_A = ref_B). Descriptive counts I ran (no truth): HG002 94 families / 473 copies: H marks 43 copies in 24 families, F 20 clusters in 10 families, both in 6 families; HG00733 174 families / 487 copies: H 7 copies in 7 families, F 5 in 5, overlap 0. |
| (g) register | Not killed as a rank. G1-G5 are killed as abstention rules (cost does not travel between donors, O2O3_EXPLORE §4.1); F alone is killed by r1304 when alleles exist; H5 has one exemplar. All three were chosen on HG002/HG00733 (development numbers). |

**Additions with prior support that O2-VC outputs lack:**
- Indel and structure columns (r1094, r1210; "real indel PSVs are ignored" is a known gap in mem:o2_variant_calls).
- Escalation of flagged families only to the IsoCon chain with the 2-transcript floor (r1203, r1363), since a flag costs only attention.
- Split-half stability to remove stochastic weak-site calls (r1336).
