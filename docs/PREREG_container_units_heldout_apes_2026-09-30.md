## 1. Question and scope

The units execution (the families input keeps every read and exon of a fusion transcript T, replaced by the exon runs between its cut introns; `PREFIX.gtf` and every assembled chain stay as they are) was built and validated on two DEV substrates and is OPT-IN. Two questions are asked of it on three substrates it has never touched, in the species where the thesis' NPIP / TBC1D3 families live:
**(i) U1, the de novo form: is it SAFE** - does it lose no more correct family members than random cuts under the same execution?
**(ii) UG, the annotation-assisted (guided) form: does it WORK** - does it keep every member BASE has and, where a copy can be recovered at all (headroom), recover more than random cuts?
**Cannot settle:** a default; any statement about human; detectors other than F1's read evidence and the annotation overlap; the 52 gorilla copy observations with no locus (units conserve exons, so they cannot create a locus); gorilla family-level truth beyond Liftoff pairs (408 labelled genes) and the two copy truths; what the units do for O2 (the copy of a fused member is its unit).

## 2. The rule (verbatim from the brief; frozen here)

```
BASE = the current defaults (arm D: f1v2 assemble + families with --min-cov-shorter 0.70), stored products reproduced as a gate (chimp: build BASE with the current defaults on the NPIP block first).
U1 (de novo units) = `RUSTLE_BRIDGE_REGROUP=f1units`, no list: F1's read evidence WITHOUT the read-share rule nominates the junctions; each bridge transcript is split into units in the families input only; gene_ids by the assembler's
  own junction-sharing locus rule inside gene_ids that hold a cut, RG3 pieces elsewhere. No annotation is read.
UG (annotation-assisted units, the guided companion) = the same execution fed `RUSTLE_BRIDGE_UNITS_LIST` from `bench/units_from_annotation.py` with the species' RefSeq GFF: every spliced transcript whose exons overlap >= 2 annotated same-strand genes with
  disjoint spans is cut at the widest intron between the last exon over the upstream gene and the first over the downstream one (readthrough records excluded).
NULL-J (3 seeds) = the SAME transcripts as UG, one random intron each, same number of cuts per transcript; NULL-T (3 seeds) = the same number of randomly chosen spliced multi-exon transcripts, random introns. Constants: none new.
```

### 2.1 Wording resolved before any arm (binding)

1. **NULL-J.** Each transcript T on UG's list (m_T cuts, almost always 1) is cut at m_T uniformly random DISTINCT introns of T (all T's introns are candidates; the UG intron is not excluded). **NULL-T.** Over UG's list rows taken in the order (m_T descending, tid ascending), the i-th row draws one transcript uniformly without replacement from POOL_T = the spliced transcripts (>= 2 exons) of the BASE `PREFIX.gtf` of the substrate that are NOT on UG's list and have >= m_T introns, and cuts it at m_T uniformly random distinct introns. NULL-T therefore has UG's number of cut transcripts and UG's multiset of cut counts, at random places; NULL-J has UG's places and UG's counts, at random introns. Neither matches the intron-position distribution (said in §8).
2. **Seeds.** `random.Random(S * 10 + j)` with S = 20263001, 20263002, 20263003 (NULL-J) and 20264001, 20264002, 20264003 (NULL-T) and j = 0 (OR6737), 1 (KB3781), 2 (PTR): one generator per (substrate, arm), transcripts and introns always taken in sorted order. The arms are named NJ1-3 and NT1-3.
3. **Lists are per contig.** `--bridge-units-list` needs every listed intron to be an intron of its transcript in the assembled GTF and the list to name at least one transcript of the run, and the port needs one region per contig, so every arm is assembled contig by contig (`--region CONTIG:0-LEN`, as the headroom run) and the per-contig families GTFs are concatenated in the contig order of §3 (the `c5.sh` of the headroom run). A contig for which an arm's list has no row is assembled in BASE's mode (`--bridge-regroup f1v2`) in that arm and its count is reported; **U1 has no list, so every contig of U1 is assembled `f1units`.**
4. **UG's transcript source** is the BASE `PREFIX.gtf` of the substrate (the "default GTF" on which the dev oracle was defined: F1v2's bridge transcripts are present, whole), through the helper with `--contigs` = the substrate's contigs and the species' RefSeq GFF (gorilla `winloci_data/GGO_genomic.gff`, chimp `winloci_data/PTR_genomic.gff`); the helper's gene set is its own (gene / pseudogene / ncRNA_gene records with exons, readthrough-described records excluded).

## 4. BASE, pools and headroom

**BASE(sigma)** = the substrate assembled contig by contig with `copy_assign --assemble-only --region CONTIG:0-LEN --assembly-junctions strict` + the driver's polish string + `--bridge-regroup f1v2 --gtf-tpm`, secondary seeding at 0.98 through the molecules table (the headroom run's `asm.sh`, the e163d955 binaries `container_headroom/bin/`: copy_assign 1325e9d1, mcl_families 67b2d40f, as_table 0452b1b9, family_score 27aa9445), concatenated; its families input is `PREFIX.families.gtf`; then the families command of §2.1.5 with no units switch.
Gates: OR6737 and KB3781 BASE are rebuilt here and reproduce the stored headroom D arm (GTFs, `clusters.tsv`, `loci.tsv`, `copies.tsv`, and the headroom fate counts of §0a through THIS test's instruments); the chimp BASE is built here, first (no stored product exists).

**Headroom (BASE-derived, fixed before any units arm).** Per block B: copies N_B; IN_B (in family under BASE: holder rule of §5.1), OUT_B (present, holder not in the family cluster: wrong family or unclustered), ABS_B (no holder). **POOL(G\*)** = the BASE loci outside the family cluster G\* (the cluster holding the most copies of B) that have >= 1 PAF record (the BASE all-vs-all; alignment block >= 300 bp at identity >= 0.70: the records `mcl_families` considers before any exon clause) onto a locus of G\*'s members (folded loci included). **HR(B) = the number of copies in OUT_B whose holder is in POOL(G\*)** = copies whose holder can be moved into the family by an operation on existing loci and their alignments. Also printed: HR0(B) = |OUT_B| (no pool condition), the number of OUT_B copies whose holder carries >= 1 UG-listed transcript touching the copy (HR_cut), and ABS_B (unreachable: no locus, and units conserve every exon base).
A block is **evaluable for the gain clause** iff HR(B) >= 1. A substrate is "with headroom" iff some block of it is evaluable. The brief's expectation (gorilla HR = 0) is checked here, not assumed.

## 5. Measures (per substrate and arm; the registered text of the brief, with the instruments named)

### 5.1 Instrument I_H: the HOLDER RULE (the headroom run's `hl_core.py`, copied unchanged, extended only to load the chimp truths)
For a copy c: holder(c) = the locus (gene_id of the arm's families input) with the most same-strand exon bp over c's territory (ties: more reads); present iff >= 1 such bp; the family cluster G\*(B, X) = the cluster that holds the holders of the most copies of block B under arm X; **c is IN FAMILY under X** iff present and its holder's cluster (through the fold map) is G\*; counts cH(X, B) = |IN_X(B)|. Reported beside: wrong family / unclustered / absent, distinct holder loci (collisions), the holders covering >= 50% of the copy (presence is not recovery: fragments count as members), family-cluster units and the units holding a copy (unit precision), partners dragged into the family cluster (units overlapping no truth copy and >= 1 annotated non-copy gene).
**lost_H(X, sigma)** = sum over the blocks of sigma of |IN_BASE(B) \ IN_X(B)| (set difference on copy ids). For a units arm the holder is a unit locus; "by consolidated locus" (the pre-split locus inherits its units' families) is reported beside for the copies whose holder is a unit.

### 5.2 Instrument I_S: the FAMILY SCORER (`family_score`, bin_base 27aa9445, `--chrom ALL --pairwise --per-family`, clusters and truth restricted to C(sigma) first, as the headroom run's `score_fam.py`)
Gene space = the species' RefSeq gene / pseudogene / ncRNA_gene records (`families_gw/species/gorilla/genes_only.gff`; for chimp the same tables built by the repository's own `figures/_o1.annotation_cache` from `PTR_genomic.gff` into scratch; genes are keyed (contig, Name), each truth gene resolved to ONE locus by `gene_at`). Truths, per species:
(a) **copy-named**: per block, the RefSeq record names of the truth copies (gorilla: the `native` field of `truth.ggo.json`; chimp T_member: the `native=` record of the copy's note when it is an NPIP record, not an `SD-partner` model; chimp T_native: the 46 names), one family per block; copies with no usable name are listed as unresolved;
(b) **Liftoff components**: connected components of genes linked by Liftoff copy pairs of the species (sequence_ID >= 0.95, both exon unions >= 200 bp, the pair's copy covering >= 50% of the gene's exon bases, readthrough records excluded: `truths.py` `_liftoff_components` of the v2 held-out run, copied; gorilla and chimpanzee `liftoff_loci.tsv`).
cS(X, B) = `hit` of block B's copy-named family; **lost_S(X, sigma)** = the number of (truth, family, gene) triples in `hit_members` under BASE and not under X, over (a) and (b). Bipartite F, sensitivity, precision and the pairwise counts are printed beside (scipy tie policy as the Rust port; the pairwise counts are tie-free); truth genes with no locus and collapsed genes are printed (the one-name-per-locus trap: a locus spanning two genes is named for one).

### 5.3 The other registered measures
- **Liftoff pairs / recall** (`lo_score.py`: pairs of the species' Liftoff copy loci, sequence_ID >= 0.95, both exon unions >= 200 bp, both loci >= 2 reads in the sample, on C(sigma); recovered when ONE family's locus covers >= 50% of each Liftoff locus; gorilla `liftoff/gorilla`, chimp `liftoff/chimpanzee`, support tables `liftoff/support/<sample>.tsv`).
- **RefSeq gene-space recall (RGS)**: the RefSeq genes of the gene space above on C(sigma), readthrough-described records excluded, that are labelled (`gene_at`, largest span overlap) by >= 1 locus that is a member of a family cluster; per arm the count, and against BASE the genes kept / lost / gained. Descriptive.
- **Family census**: clusters (>= 2 members), members (cluster rows), largest cluster, loci (nodes of the families input), edges and largest connected component of the dumped graph, the family cluster of each block (size).
- **CORRECT MEMBERS LOST vs BASE**: lost_H and lost_S above, per substrate, per arm; also by block and by (truth, family).
- **Safety census**: cuts, cut transcripts, units; single-exon units and units under 600 exonic bp; **single genes cut** (cut transcripts whose exons overlap exactly one annotated same-strand non-readthrough gene; the distinct genes) and the SEP / FRAG / UNJ class of each cut transcript (the RG3 `cls.py` definitions on the units of T: SEP = no annotated gene shared by two units and every unit overlaps a gene, FRAG = some gene is overlapped by >= 2 units, UNJ = no gene shared and some unit overlaps none); cut transcripts overlapping a truth copy and copies touched by a cut; **units failing gates the fused locus passed** (units whose arm locus is unclustered while the BASE locus of the parent transcript is clustered; with whether any PAF record >= 300 bp at identity >= 0.70 exists for the locus); **hub rate** (nodes of the dumped graph of degree >= 10 / nodes; and the unit loci among them; BASE beside); **relation records by outcome** (SAME / DIFF / ONE_UNCL / ALL_UNCL from `fam.relations.tsv`) and **`cover` precision against annotation parents** (a DIFF record is annotation-supported iff every unit overlaps >= 1 annotated gene and the units' gene sets are pairwise disjoint; single-gene iff the union is one gene; other otherwise: supported / (cover records)); `members_by_locus` double counts.
- **Runtime and memory**: wall and peak RSS of each contig's assemble (summed) and of the families run (`/usr/bin/time -v`).

## 6. Clauses and verdicts (integer comparisons; fixed now)

Arms X in {U1, UG}; NULL arms N in {NJ1, NJ2, NJ3, NT1, NT2, NT3} (six seeds); instruments i in {H, S}; substrates sigma in {OR6737, KB3781, PTR}; blocks B as in §3.
- **H1 (harm).** lost_i(X, sigma) <= min over the six NULL arms of lost_i(N, sigma), for EVERY substrate and BOTH instruments (six conditions). Reported per condition; **informative** iff the largest NULL loss in that condition is >= 1 (a condition in which no arm loses anything holds vacuously and is labelled so, never counted as evidence of safety).
- **H3 (copies; I_H decides, I_S guards).** (a) cH(X, B) >= cH(BASE, B) for every block B (no copy lost against BASE), and cS(X, B) >= cS(BASE, B) for every block whose copy-named truth has >= 2 resolved genes; (b) on every block with HR(B) >= 1: cH(X, B) > the maximum over the six NULL arms of cH(N, B) (strictly more copies in the family than every seed). **H3 holds** iff (a) holds on every block and (b) holds on every block with HR >= 1 and at least one block has HR >= 1. **H3 is NOT EVALUABLE** iff no block has HR >= 1 (not failed: (a) is still reported and a loss still fails it). The gorilla expectation HR = 0 is a prediction, §7.
- **H4 (U1 safe).** U1 is **SAFE** iff H1 holds for U1, else **UNSAFE**.
- **H5 (relations, descriptive).** Relation records by outcome, `cover` precision, double counts, per arm; no bar.
- **Verdict UG.** **WORKS** iff H1(UG) and H3(UG) (H3 on at least one evaluable block: a substrate with headroom); **PARTIAL** iff H1(UG) and not H3(UG) (including H3 NOT EVALUABLE); **NOT** iff not H1(UG). **Verdict U1:** SAFE / UNSAFE. The verdicts are read substrate by substrate in the tables (a per-substrate H1 row is printed for each arm), and the registered verdict is the aggregate above.
- **A units arm that does not complete** on a substrate is H1-failed for that arm (an unsafe execution), with its error reported; a NULL arm that fails for an infrastructure reason is re-run once; a deterministic failure of a NULL list is a generator defect and a deviation.
- **Nothing here is a default decision.** WORKS would justify a held-out confirmation on a second library of a species with headroom and nothing more; SAFE would justify keeping U1 opt-in as a relation channel, not a default.

## 8. Hostile self-review

1. **Zero headroom makes every arm equal.** If HR = 0 on every block, UG = U1 = NULL = BASE on every copy count by construction (units conserve exons; an absent copy cannot gain a locus); H3 is NOT EVALUABLE and the verdict reduces to H1, which then tests harm only. The expected gorilla verdict is PARTIAL at best, and the test is chiefly a SAFETY test on three fresh substrates; a PARTIAL here is not a gain.
2. **H1 can hold vacuously.** When no arm (NULLs included) loses any correct member, "lost <= min null" is 0 <= 0. The informative flag prints this; a SAFE built only on vacuous conditions is a statement that nothing could be detected.
3. **Fragment reads and presence-not-recovery.** 49 of 50 gorilla copy-samples have no read spanning the chain and 18 of 19 present copies are 3' fragments (r1148 line): a unit holding the copy's terminal exon counts as "in family" under I_H (presence). The holder-coverage column and the "by consolidated locus" reading are printed so a fragment unit is visible; they are not clauses.
4. **Absent copies.** 52 of 78 gorilla observations (and an unknown number in chimp) have no locus: no execution on existing loci can recover them. They bound every gain from above and are printed per block.
5. **NPIPB-clade fusions with SMG1P / RRN3P1 / EIF3C-like partners.** UG cuts these at the annotated partner boundary; the NPIP-side unit may be a terminal fragment that fails the shared-exon gate (`--min-shared-exon-frac 0.60`) or the aligner floor, and the partner unit may land in the partner's family (SMG1-like) or, through the SD homology, in NPIP (the partner-dragging mechanism of the mechanism test). Partners dragged into the family cluster are censused per arm; a copy lost that way is Z1, not a rule failure of the test.
6. **U1 over-cutting.** F1 without its share rule cuts annotated single genes (about 40 per sample on gorilla; the human A119b over-cut): Z7 and the single-gene census measure it; the units keep every read and exon, so over-cutting costs nodes (hubs, tiny units), not chains.
7. **The NULLs are matched on counts, not on positions.** NULL-J matches UG's places and cut counts, NULL-T its transcript count and cut-count multiset; neither matches the distribution of introns (UG cuts the widest intron between two genes, a random intron is typically short) nor the unit sizes. A NULL that does worse than UG may be doing worse because its units are tiny, not because the junction is wrong; the unit-size census is printed per arm.
8. **Six NULL seeds make "<= min" a conservative harm bar and "> max" a high gain bar.** One seed that loses nothing sets H1's bar at zero for X; one seed that gains one copy fails H3(b) with HR small. Both are the brief's clauses; the per-seed values are printed.
9. **Two instruments disagree by design.** The holder rule maps a copy to one locus by exon overlap; the scorer maps a NAME to one locus by span overlap and cannot name a copy that has no usable RefSeq record (chimp T_member copies landing on an SD-partner model, unresolved). The clauses require both to agree where defined (H1 and H3(a)); a disagreement is Z6.
10. **Truths are proxies.** T_member is the 2026-09-17 proxy (liftoff AND identity landing agree); T_native is the species' own NPIP-described RefSeq records (46 chimp records include pseudogene and fragment records); the Liftoff components cover recent duplications at identity >= .95. A "correct member" is correct against these, not against a gold standard; no Compara exists for gorilla or chimp.
11. **One animal per sample, three samples, one library each.** KB3781 is the assembly's own individual and carries the reference's haplotypes (its reads align better), OR6737 and PTR are other animals; the three substrates differ in depth, copy truth and annotation completeness, so a rule that holds on two and fails on one is reported as such, never averaged.
12. **Dev exposure.** NC_073244.2 is F1's gorilla development contig and holds a T_member copy; the F1 evidence (U1's detector) was designed with it. It is kept in the decision as the brief names the contig and printed without it beside. The F1 junction lists on the gorilla samples are known (§0b); only the families-stage outcome is unseen.
13. **Heavy runs through a shard wrapper and per-contig assembly.** The families all-vs-all is the byte-identical sharded minimap2; the assembly is per contig (validated equal to the stored genome-wide GTF on these contigs by the headroom gate). A difference from the driver's `--genome-wide` assemble would be an input difference and is gated (G3, G4).
14. **The chimp BASE is new.** Its first build is also the first current-default chimp product; a BASE surprise (a gate failure or a different family) is reported before any arm and does not change a clause.
15. **What this cannot say:** that a real de novo detector exists for the dominant links (register 1166D: 0 of the real F1v2 bridges joined two multi-copy families on the human chromosomes; the dev R / R2 gains were two copies on a simulation); anything about orangutan (not opened) or human (sibling test); anything about O2.
