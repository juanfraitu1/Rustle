## O2 (`o3port.py run --semi --guard`)
- Exact tie (same AS and edit distance on the two best copies): HOPELESS (abstain).
- Semi-tied: best copy only with a lead of >= 2 distinguishing sites, each carried by >= 6 family reads at that column
  (MIN_SUPPORT) and flanked by 5 gap-free aligned bases in both alignments (SITE_FLANK); else abstain.
- `--guard`: abstain on every tied read touching a copy where O3 flags a putative copy.
- Held-outs: P12 PASS (`docs/archive/2026-10/merged/PREREG_o2_semi_sites_p12_2026-10-24.md`, 4,562 placed, 0.02% wrong); P13 FAIL S2 by 2 reads
  (`docs/archive/2026-10/merged/PREREG_o2_guard_p13_2026-10-24.md`, indel-shifted sites); P14 PASS (`docs/archive/2026-10/merged/PREREG_o2_guard_p14_2026-10-24.md`, 0.12% wrong,
  0 on the lacked copy, flagged extra-copy runs abstain). Limit: an unflagged extra copy (8 / 30 flagged) leaves O2 ~40% wrong.
- Real reads (refabsent panel, `docs/archive/2026-10/merged/REPORT_o2_refabsent_2026-10-24.md`): 157 placed, 0 wrong vs minimap2 MAPQ >= 1 29 / 418.
- Not adopted (opt-in): `--prefer` (1393), `--one-site` (1399, unsafe under gene conversion), `--anchor` (1400, 4 / 11 wrong on real

## Since 1401 (2026-10-24, registers 1402-1408)
- **Rust port** `copy_phasing` (`src/copy_phasing/`, binary `copy_phasing`; `PORT=rs` in `bench/o3_graph/o3port_p4.sh`): byte-identical
  to `o3port.py` on 81 golden cases at 1 and 4 threads (1402).
- **The advisor's "secondary AS >= primary + 10 -> assign" rule is refuted** (1403, real fibroblast BAM): it fires on 399,865 reads, and
  99.9% of those secondaries have fewer introns (96.4% unspliced vs a spliced primary). splice-mode AS charges each intron, so a
  processed copy outscores the true spliced one. Held-out simulation P15 PASS (1404): the rule's secondary is right 2.0% of the time,
  the primary 99.6%.
- **Tie window `--tie-structure`** (same intron count + AS within 10), opt-in in both ports (1405). Genome-wide it shrinks the tied set
  469k -> 41k reads. In O2 it changes nothing: P16 uninformative (1406, O2 compares only a family's own copies); on the real P5 / P10
  panels the tied rows and assignments are identical (1407). It matters for the copy finder: P10 gains a false flag, GWFAM257, a real
  maternal allele. Default unchanged until the copy finder separates a divergent allele from an extra copy.
- **How far Jim is from his own reference** (1408, `LEDGER_O3.md`): the reference is a mosaic of KB3781's two haplotypes
  (1,414 / 1,415 catalog copies identical in mat or pat). Autosomal copy count differs between mat and pat in 27-35 / 494 families

  families with a reference-absent copy in 1401, GWFAM4, 70 and 175 also differ in mat/pat copy count here; GWFAM103 does not.

## Since 1408 (2026-10-25, registers 1409-1415)
- **minimap2 ranks hits by `ms`, not AS** (1409; hit.c / align.c): primary = max `ms` in 99.3% of real fibroblast reads with a
  secondary; AS disagrees with the primary in 9.9%.
- **No read-level score decides near-ties** (P18 FAIL, 1410): an error-model likelihood agrees with `ms` on 220 / 222 decidable reads;
  its confident calls are wrong on 40 / 220, all reads from the other allele. No tag or CIGAR feature helps (1411, dev only).
  Pooled abundance (EM / Gibbs) gives a probability, not an assignment (1412, dev only).
- **copy_phasing on both of Jim's alleles** (P21 FAIL, 1413): O2 assigns 9 tied reads (all right) but fixes none of the 40 allele
  errors; 2 false certified copies, causes in 1414 (one fixed by `--tie-structure`).
- **`--certify-all-records`** (opt-in, both ports, 122 golden cases): a certified copy must survive a slot matching over every family
  copy its reads have any record on. P22 (1415) FAIL: removes no false copy on a masked-copy panel (1 false in every arm) and loses
  3 / 11 true ones. Certified recall there is 11-13 / 70 masked copies.
- **Copy number at the SD and exon level** (1416, report, `LEDGER_O3.md`): between Jim's own two haplotypes, copy number differs in
  44.8% of 5 kb SD windows (method noise 6.6%) and in 7.4% of exons (noise 0.2%); 24% of our families have an exon whose count
  differs, against 5-7% for whole gene copies (1408). Reference bias in copy content is real at the unit SD studies use.
- **Breakpoint groups do not find them** (1417, BPCLUST FAIL): clusters of split / clipped reads at junctions absent from the
  reference flag 7 / 75 held-out families with a differing exon count against 11 / 244 without (precision 0.39, p 0.10).
- **Nor does the certified tier run per exon** (1418, EXHAP FAIL): certified exon units flag 7 held-out families, 2 of 51 with an
  exon gain (precision 0.29). About two-thirds of the extra exon copies match an existing reference copy over the exon, so their
  reads form no new haplotype. Putative exon units are enriched for gains (3 / 9 on dev and on held-out) but rare.
- **Jim's WGS confirms the large differences, not the small ones** (1419): reads side with the assemblies in 94% (SD) / 77%
  (exons) of units differing by >= 25%, but only 63-72% of +-1 differences in high-copy units (12x cannot resolve them). The
  WGS-supported core: 3.0% of SD windows, exon differences in 6.3% of families. 1416's headline is an upper bound.
- **RNA could see much more than the detector finds** (1420): with the true haplotype sequences, 41.9% of exon gains have >= 2
  real reads closer to a haplotype-only sequence than to any reference copy (control 14%). The per-exon certified tier finds 2-4%
  (1418), so the detector is the bottleneck; likely cause: the extra copy takes a free allele slot (putative, not certified).
