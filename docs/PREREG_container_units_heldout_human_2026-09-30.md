## 1. Question and scope

The units execution realises the fused-locus container as a UNIT SPLIT in the families input: a transcript that joins two things is replaced, in `PREFIX.families.gtf` only, by the exon runs between its cut introns, and gene_ids are re-derived by the assembler's own junction-sharing locus rule where a cut was made. Two forms:
**U1** (de novo: F1's read evidence nominates the junctions, no annotation read) and **UG** (annotation-assisted, the guided companion: every spliced transcript over >= 2 annotated genes is cut between them). On RESERVED human data:
(i) is UG HARMLESS (correct members not lost beyond matched random cuts, Compara pairs not below BASE) and does it GAIN (Compara TP pairs above BASE and above every matched null) and keep the NPIP / TBC1D3 copies; (ii) is U1 SAFE and does it gain.
**Cannot settle:** any default flip (the user's call); gorilla and chimp (sibling study); a detector for dominant links (register 1166D); O2; cross-chromosome copies (A119b families are per chromosome, as the stored arm D); families of one gene (Compara is universe-limited).

## 2. The rule (verbatim from the brief; frozen before any units arm exists)

```
BASE = the current defaults (arm D: f1v2 assemble + families with --min-cov-shorter 0.70), stored products reproduced as a gate.
U1 (de novo units) = `RUSTLE_BRIDGE_REGROUP=f1units`, no list: F1's read evidence WITHOUT the read-share rule nominates the junctions; each bridge transcript is split into units in the families input only; gene_ids by the
  assembler's own junction-sharing locus rule inside gene_ids that hold a cut, RG3 pieces elsewhere. No annotation is read.
UG (annotation-assisted units, the guided companion) = the same execution fed `RUSTLE_BRIDGE_UNITS_LIST` from `bench/units_from_annotation.py` with the CHM13 RefSeq full GFF (never HSA_genomic.gff): every spliced transcript whose exons overlap
  >= 2 annotated same-strand genes with disjoint spans is cut at the widest intron between the last exon over the upstream gene and the first over the downstream one (readthrough records excluded).
NULL-J (3 seeds) = the SAME transcripts as UG, one random intron each, same number of cuts per transcript; NULL-T (3 seeds) = the same number of randomly chosen spliced multi-exon transcripts of the sample, random introns.
Constants: none new (F1's inherited evidence constants only).
```

A list arm does NOT read F1's evidence, so **F1v2's bridge removal is not part of a list arm**: BASE (f1v2) removes the F1v2 bridge transcripts from its families input, UG / NULL arms keep them (cut where listed). The number of cut transcripts and cuts per contig equals UG's; a contig whose pool is too small is reported as a shortfall.

6. **Which nulls judge which arm.** The six seeds (NULL-J 1-3, NULL-T 1-3) are UG-sized and are the nulls of every clause for BOTH UG and U1 (literal text). 9. **"Pairs"** in H1 / H2 = TRUE-POSITIVE predicted pairs (set semantics, `family_score --pairwise` `tp`), pooled over the sample's scored contigs (A119b: the 18-chromosome pooled truth; testis: genome-wide minus DEV contigs); per-chromosome rows use the per-chromosome truth (families with >= 2 genes on that chromosome); the sum of per-chromosome TP equals the pooled TP (checked on BASE).

## 4. Measures (definitions binding; none introduces a threshold)

### 4.1 The two label readings (Compara pairs)
Gene space and truth exactly as `o1_cover_frozen/score.py` / `family_score --chrom ALL`: gene = (contig, Name) of a `gene` / `pseudogene` / `ncRNA_gene` record of `families_gw/species/human/genes_only.gff`; Compara Primates families (first family per gene, truth families with >= 2 genes present in the scored contigs; universe U = their genes);
a cluster member is a locus key (contig, start, end) of `clusters.tsv`, labelled by `gene_at` (largest span overlap, first maximum in sorted order); a predicted family's gene set = its members' labels in U; bipartite = scipy `linear_sum_assignment` on |T n X| (the binary's tie policy; pairwise counts are tie-free and decide the clauses).
**STRICT** = the member's own `gene_at` label: the frozen `family_score` binary 7723029b and the Python re-implementation (gate G2 requires equality of every printed number).
**PRIMARY** (the o1_cover / CSM "benefit of the doubt" for a member that carries more than one gene, extended to every member and monotone): the family's set is the STRICT set plus, for every member whose own label is not in its cluster's PLURALITY truth family (plurality over the STRICT labels in U, ties by family id), ONE extra label: the first gene in U of that plurality family
overlapped on the member's strand by >= 1 exon base of the member's REPRESENTATIVE transcript (largest exon overlap first; the representative = the families stage's own rule, read from the arm's families GTF; strand-less representatives get none). Labels are only ever added; pairs are set pairs. PRIMARY answers "does the split find family members beyond what a collision-free namer would credit the unsplit locus for?" (the scorer's one-name-per-locus trap, headroom §4.2).
Both readings are printed for every arm, per chromosome and pooled, with sens / prec / F, truth / predicted / TP pairs, clusters scored, collapsed genes and members outside the truth universe (register 991 / 992 trap: predictions are intersected with the universe, so an arm that adds unlabelled members is not penalised; the counts are reported beside).

### 4.2 Correct members lost vs BASE, by BOTH instruments (the instrument is named in every count)
- **Scorer instrument (`family_score`):** a Compara gene is a HIT in an arm iff it is in `hit_members` of the cluster the binary matches to its truth family (`--per-family`, STRICT labels, pooled run of the sample); a CORRECT MEMBER of BASE = a hit under BASE; LOST by an arm = a BASE hit that is not a hit under the arm (gene level, counted once).
- **Holder-rule instrument (`hl_core.holder_of`, the headroom fate table):** per Compara multi-copy gene (record not readthrough-described; family with >= 2 genes on the scored contig, per chromosome for A119b; genome-wide minus DEV contigs for testis) the locus with the most same-strand exon overlap; `right family` = its cluster is the cluster that holds most holders of that truth family (unit loci of a split transcript are loci like any other); a CORRECT MEMBER of BASE = fate `right family` under BASE; LOST = `right family` under BASE and any other fate under the arm.
  The two instruments disagree on single genes (headroom §3.7); both are required in H1. Both are reproduced from the stored D products (gate G1: the holder counts equal the headroom §4.3 table per chromosome; the scorer hits equal the binary's `--per-family`).

### 4.3 NPIP / TBC1D3 copies in their family (testis, holder rule)
The 26 Dishuck NPIP copies (chr16) and the 16 RefSeq TBC1D3 records (chr17) of `copy_recovery_tools/ann/truth.hsa.json`, holder rule of §4.2, `right family` = holder in the cluster holding most copies of the family. **Headroom (stated now):** BASE testis NPIP 5 of 26 right, 2 wrong, 19 with no locus (the oracle: 5 / 5; the wrong-family copies NPIPB4 and NPIPB5 are 529-bp single-exon fragments with no alignment to the family), TBC1D3 0 of 16 (all 16 have no locus); so **H3 cannot show a gain, it can only fail by losing a copy**.

### 4.4 Descriptive measures (no clause)
Liftoff copy-pair recall (`lo_score.py`, rows = members' representative exon unions); the family scorer's collapsed / no-locus counts; **RefSeq gene-space recall** = distinct non-readthrough RefSeq genes that are the `gene_at` label of >= 1 locus of the arm (all loci; and clustered loci only) over the RefSeq genes with >= 1 exon base overlapped, same strand, by >= 1 transcript of the plain assembly (the same denominator for every arm of a sample);
protein-homology **referee F** where a referee exists (A119b chr2, chr6, chr8, chr10 and the same chromosomes of testis; `fig7/current/human_<chr>_ref.families.tsv`, its GFF, the frozen binary, STRICT); runtime and peak RSS of `assemble` and `families` (`/usr/bin/time -v`).

### 4.5 Safety census (per arm; descriptive, no bar)
cuts, cut transcripts, units, single-exon units (and how many attach to a neighbouring component), cut transcripts lying inside ONE annotated gene (exon overlap with exactly one non-readthrough record: "single genes cut by annotation"), **units failing the gates the fused locus passed** (a unit locus unclustered whose input gene's BASE locus is clustered),
hub rate (unit loci in nodes of degree >= 10 of the admitted-edge dump, beside the share of all nodes with degree >= 10), relation records by outcome (`SAME` / `DIFF` / `ONE_UNCL` / `ALL_UNCL`, from `--emit-relations`, Rust-built arms) and the **`cover` records' precision against the annotation parents** (a `cover` record is annotation-supported iff its units overlap >= 2 non-empty pairwise-disjoint gene sets, single-gene iff one gene, other otherwise) and against the **Compara parents** (supported iff every judged unit's cluster holds a Compara mate of the unit's gene; judgeable iff >= 2 units have a gene in U).

## 5. Clauses (integer comparisons) and verdict mapping

The brief's clause text, verbatim:

```
CLAUSES (integer comparisons; write the numbers' meaning in the prereg):
H1 harm: correct members lost by U1 / UG <= the smallest NULL seed's lost count on the same instrument AND pooled Compara pairs >= BASE;
H2 gain (UG): pooled Compara TP pairs (PRIMARY and STRICT) UG > BASE and > every NULL seed, and UG >= BASE on more chromosomes than it is below;
H3 copies: NPIP / TBC1D3 copies in family UG >= BASE on testis (state the headroom);
H4 U1: SAFE iff H1 holds; GAIN iff also H2 holds for U1;
H5 relations (descriptive, no bar): `cover` records of UG and U1 versus annotation / Compara parents.
Verdicts: UG WORKS = H1+H2+H3, PARTIAL = H1 + (H2 or H3), else NOT; U1 SAFE / UNSAFE and GAIN / NO GAIN.
```

### 5.1 The clauses with their numbers' meaning
Notation: TP_L(X, S) = pooled TP pairs of arm X on sample S under label reading L in {STRICT, PRIMARY}; LOST_I(X, S) = correct members lost by X vs BASE on S by instrument I in {scorer, holder}; N = the six UG-sized null arms (NJ1-3, NT1-3).
- **H1(X, S)** (harm) holds iff LOST_scorer(X, S) <= min over N of LOST_scorer AND LOST_holder(X, S) <= min over N of LOST_holder AND TP_STRICT(X, S) >= TP_STRICT(BASE, S) AND TP_PRIMARY(X, S) >= TP_PRIMARY(BASE, S). **H1(X)** = H1(X, A119b) and H1(X, testis).
  Meaning: X loses no more correct members than the BEST random-cut arm and does not lower the pooled TP pairs under either reading. Ties pass (`<=`, `>=`); a count of 0 lost passes whatever the nulls do.
- **H2(X)** (gain, A119b) holds iff for BOTH readings L: TP_L(X) > TP_L(BASE) AND TP_L(X) > TP_L(n) for every n in N, AND the number of the 18 chromosomes with TP_L(X, c) >= TP_L(BASE, c) is larger than the number with TP_L(X, c) < TP_L(BASE, c) (per-chromosome truth; ties count as `>=`, so an unchanged chromosome counts for X; the numbers above / equal / below are printed).
  Meaning: a sign test on integers: +1 pair above BASE and above the best null passes; the size is reported, not required. **Headroom (stated now):** the oracle of the headroom report realised +24 STRICT TP pairs on the 19-chromosome pool with only chr1 (+27) and chr19 (-3) split, and part of that gain is the scorer's own collision count (names made exact, headroom §4.2); a UG gain far above +24 on the 18 chromosomes is therefore suspect (falsifier Z4). Under PRIMARY BASE already carries 633 TP pairs against STRICT's 517 (18 chromosomes), so the PRIMARY room for a gain is smaller.
- **H3(UG)** (copies, testis) holds iff NPIP copies `right family` under UG >= BASE (5) AND TBC1D3 copies `right family` under UG >= BASE (0). Meaning: no copy is lost; the oracle gains none, so the clause is a harm test (§4.3).
- **H4(U1):** SAFE iff H1(U1); GAIN iff H1(U1) and H2(U1); otherwise UNSAFE (H1 fails) / NO GAIN (H2 fails).
- **H5:** the relation tables of UG and U1 (§4.5), no bar.

### 5.2 Verdicts (binding reading, §2.1 item 7)
**UG WORKS** iff H1(UG) and H2(UG) and H3(UG); **PARTIAL** iff H1(UG) and (H2(UG) or H3(UG)) and not WORKS; **NOT** otherwise. **U1:** SAFE / UNSAFE by H1(U1), GAIN / NO GAIN by H2(U1) (GAIN needs SAFE).
### 5.3 Per-sample table
Every H1 and H2 sub-clause is printed per sample, per instrument, per reading and per null seed, so any other reading of the brief's text can be recomputed from the Outcome.
### 5.4 Strict reading beside every verdict
"H2 required on testis too" (UG > BASE and > every null under both readings on the genome-wide testis pool) is printed beside the binding verdict; if it differs from the binding verdict both are stated.

## 7. Hostile self-review

1. **Single-exon units and hubs.** A unit of one exon aligns promiscuously and becomes a hub (dev chr16 oracle: 122 of 509 units single-exon, 93 in degree >= 10 nodes; U1 on A119b: 835 single-exon of 13,009 units). The census (§4.5) reports them, the clauses do not penalise them; a Compara gain can coexist with a hub flood the metrics do not see.
2. **SD-mosaic loci (NBPF, GOLGA, ANKRD, GUSBP, ZNF, NOTCH2NL).** The Compara families of these loci are where fused loci hold members (chr1: the NOTCH2NL | NBPF joins; the oracle gained +27 TP pairs on chr1 and lost 3 on chr19) and where one locus can decide a chromosome; per-chromosome rows and the loci behind the largest differences are reported; a result carried by one chromosome is stated as such.
3. **Over-cutting by U1.** F1's evidence without the share rule cut 6,037 transcripts into 13,009 units on A119b (all 19 chromosomes) and fragments single genes (the F1-on-human failure: 451 fragmenting vs 219 separating splits). Against UG-sized nulls the literal H1 is harder for U1 (it cuts about 3x more); the U1-sized null (beside, optional) is the fair comparator for that reading.
4. **Unjudgeable Compara labels.** Compara is universe-limited (BASE pooled: 5,714 of 6,334 members have a label outside the universe); an arm that adds unlabelled members is not penalised (register 991 / 992) and a gain can be annotation coverage; the counts of members outside U are printed and the holder-rule instrument (which sees every Compara gene) is the counterweight.
5. **List arms are a different execution from BASE.** UG and the nulls do not remove F1v2's bridge transcripts; UG versus BASE mixes that with the cut positions, UG versus the nulls isolates the positions. The optional beside-arm NAT0 (the same execution with no cut, built by the Python prototype) decomposes it; it is descriptive only.
6. **The list reads the annotation that also builds the truth.** RefSeq gene names label both the cut positions and the Compara truth; cutting at annotated gene boundaries makes unit loci map to exactly one gene and the scorer then names them exactly (collapsed genes fall). NULL-J controls for cut count and transcripts, NOT for this; PRIMARY and the collapsed counts are the controls, and the oracle's headroom (+24) bounds what is plausible.
7. **Per-chromosome families hide cross-chromosome copies.** The pooled truth holds 352 cross-chromosome pairs (63 families) that A119b's per-chromosome families can never contain (pair sensitivity capped at 2,394 / 2,746 = 87.2% on the 19-chromosome pool); nothing here can change that; testis is genome-wide.
8. **The sign test is cheap.** H2 passes on +1 pair; H3 passes on a tie (and TBC1D3 has no locus to lose); the sizes and per-chromosome rows are reported so a +1 "gain" is not read as one.
9. **Two samples of one species, one annotation.** A119b and testis share the RefSeq annotation, the Compara truth and the stored pipeline; testis has few cuts and the NPIP / TBC1D3 copies have zero headroom there; A119b is the decisive sample for H2.
10. **chr16 / chr17 are DEV and excluded**; chr13, 18, 20-22 are not in the stored arm D. The 18-chromosome universe is the stored one minus chr17, not a re-derivation.
11. **The NULL builders.** NULL-J keeps UG's transcripts (and so UG's gene-pair structure), NULL-T uses transcripts that need not overlap two genes at all; a UG advantage over NULL-T is expected and weak evidence, over NULL-J strong.
12. **Iteration log of the choices made on BASE alone (honest list).** PRIMARY went through two versions on the stored D products before any units arm existed: (1) parents = every gene whose SPAN overlaps the member's locus span (strand-blind): it credited multi-gene hub loci so generously that BASE gained +208 TP pairs on the 19-chromosome pool (561 -> 769, +37%); (2) the final one, parents = genes overlapped on the member's strand by exons of its representative: +121 (561 -> 682, +21%; largest on chr7, chr10, chr15 and chrX). Version (2) was chosen because the family's member IS its representative transcript; the choice was not tuned against any units result.
