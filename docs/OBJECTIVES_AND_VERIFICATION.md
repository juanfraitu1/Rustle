## Final note — how to use this file

Quote **the conclusions at the top**, with their limits attached, and use the rows below only to answer
"how do you know that?". Three rules survive every pass here and should govern anything added later:

1. **Never quote a rate whose denominator the method controls.** Seven metrics have died on this project
   for that reason, the most recent (`detector.rate()`) after it had already reversed the sign of a
   published result. The fixed-denominator form is the headline; the called-rate is a diagnostic.
2. **Never quote a number without its substrate.** Human ≠ gorilla, and the gorilla substrate is
   **cross-individual**. Pooled figures are mixtures and must be labelled as such.
3. **Separate PROVEN from MEASURED out loud.** P1 seed-invariance and the O2 decomposition are theorems
   and cannot be argued with; every rate on this page is only as good as one human and one gorilla
   dataset, and saying so is what makes the theorems credible.
