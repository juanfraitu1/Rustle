Copies with a node: 24, 24, 24, 24, 25, 25, 25; the one extra copy is PKD1P6-NPIPP1, whose node exists under the registered NPIPP1 exons and already exists at P under the Liftoff PKD1P6-NPIPP1 definition of the same copy table (two coordinate definitions of one copy), so it is not evidence about the pool.
2. **The family-level scores are contig-wide and do not show the NPIP family gaining.** Compara, U2 and Soto are scored over all their families on the contig (chr16: Soto 20 families / 71 genes, Compara 17 / 54). The Soto gain from P to A (hits 30 to 52 of 71; sensitivity .423 to .732, bipartite F .588 to .832) comes from the other 18 families (17 to 39 of 51 hits); the NPIP-holding Soto families ID_154 and ID_149 contribute 13, 14, 13 hits at P, G98, A, Compara CF153 is 13 of 19 at P, G98 and A, U2 ID_154 is 14, 15, 14 of 19 (target rows below). The pairwise F, which is tie-free, moves less than the bipartite F (Soto P to A +.032 against +.244; Compara .650 to .636).
3. **TBC1D3 (chr17, 16 truth copies, ceiling 15 because TBC1D3P7 has no exons in the truth; 11 expressed) is a different shape: the default is ahead of primary-only on the family and behind it on CP*.** G98 against P: exact representatives 8 against 7 (one copy, TBC1D3 itself; M2n the same), copies in the largest cluster 11 against 9, copies with a node and locus-level found tied (13, 9), NP 1.000 against .812 but CP* .59 against .81 (1.7 against 1.0 on-copy nodes per copy), Soto precision .725 against .852, PAF records +7.3%. 4. **0.98 is neither a knee nor a plateau.** At NPIP M2 changes by -2, -2, 0, -2, -3, -1 along ρ = 1.0, .995, .98, .95, .90 and A (unequal steps with a flat stretch at the default); the registered claim that G95 and G995 stay within one copy of the default failed (S8: G95 has 6 against 8) and the first loss is already at exact ties (G100: NPIPB5 and LOC124907807 lose their exact representative). At TBC1D3 M1 and M2 stay within one copy (S8 held). Earlier family-level work found the width 0.98 to 0.90 insensitive on other substrates (register 1060, 1100); the representative count is sensitive to it.
5. **A primary-first representative (Rule 1) repairs the representative at NPIP G98 and A, at a family-F cost, and does other things elsewhere.** NPIP G98: 8 to 11 (NPIPA8, NPIPB15 and LOC124907807 return; M2n 7 to 10), A: 2 to 9 (+8, and NPIPB10P is lost; M2n 1 to 5); copies with a node and the largest-cluster count unchanged at G98 and A, although the node sets differ (G98: 2 leave and 2 enter; A: 12 leave, 20 enter); 21 of 2,832 representatives change at G98 and 75 of 5,951 at A. Elsewhere: NPIP G90+R1 loses PKD1P6-NPIPP1's node (M1 25 to 24, M6 24 to 22); TBC1D3 G100+R1 loses a node and an exact representative, G995+R1 changes the node set (13 against 19 nodes), G95+R1 gains a representative, G98+R1 changes no count, A+R1 raises copies with a node 12 to 15 and Soto F .599 to .631. P+R1 is identical to P.
6. **Registered non-dominated arms on (copies with a node, exact representatives, NP).** NPIP, primary arms: P, A and A+R1, but A is non-dominated only through an NP larger by .002 (69/168 against 72/176; at two decimals A+R1 dominates A); the whole sweep: P, P+R1 and G95+R1 (25 / 11 / .733 against 24 / 12 / .725; single copies, a sweep reading, not a registered claim). TBC1D3, primary arms: G98, G98+R1 and A+R1. The statistic does not name a winner.
7. **What NP, the family scores and M2 can and cannot see.** NP counts every locus on a truth copy as correct, so loci piled on a copy raise it (the register's earlier read-pool study counted up to 25 loci at one copy under `all`); its denominator is the arm's own family clusters, so a rise can be re-clustering (at TBC1D3 the three off-copy nodes of P still exist at G98 as singletons or in non-family clusters, they left the denominator without being removed). `family_score` deletes predicted members outside the truth universe and labels a locus by its largest-overlap gene regardless of strand: at chr16 A has 99 nodes on no truth copy (21 in-span, 32 antisense, 46 elsewhere), 67 to 68 of them labelled by no gene of the three universes (invisible) and 31 to 32 scored (some credited), so the contig-wide family precision understates the cost of `all` without ignoring it (Compara 1.000 to .857, U2 .760 to .629, Soto .968 to .963 against NP .725 to .411). The on-copy test uses the exon union of the copy's truth transcripts, which is the whole span for three TBC1D3 copies (TBC1D3B, TBC1D3F, LOC100420311) and for about 8 of the 24 gorilla NPIP copies; without those three TBC1D3 copies M1 13 becomes 10 and NP .81 and 1.00 become .62 and .77. M2 is the instrument's count: the copy has an own node and some same-strand locus overlapping it, node or not, has the exact representative; M2n restricts the exact locus to nodes (the 10-06 re-score's 'strict FOUND within own nodes 8 of 25' has the same reading as M2).
8. **Gorilla (section 5): the chains the all pool builds at the expressed NPIP copies are largely other copies' reads, so it does not show that those copies are expressed or that the primary pool is the cause.** Outside the all pool no locus carries any annotated intron of the 9 expressed copies; under the all pool 7 of 9 carry 2 to 4 annotated introns and 22 of 24 copies get a node (11 otherwise), but 93% of the secondary alignments that carry the introns belong to molecules whose primary alignment lies in another NPIP copy. Rule 1 changes no registered count; gorilla TBC1D3 is underpowered. The all-secondaries arms were deferred at the user's request (Amendment 2): the NPIP arm had finished and is reported, A and A+R1 on TBC1D3 and A+R1 on NPIP are not run.

## Reading

- The pool decision is a position on a trade-off, not a free improvement. At NPIP primary-only is ahead on the exact representative, NP and CP* and level on membership and cohesion; the default is ahead on the contig-wide family recall measures (mainly other families) and level on the NPIP family's own rows. At TBC1D3 the default recovers more of the family (CF185 9 of 9 against 5) at a higher number of loci per copy. `all` is expensive (22.9 times the aligner records at chr16) and splits the NPIP family; the gain it shows in contig-wide scores is not the target family's.
- The right width is not obvious from these counts: the representative count falls steadily while NP, cohesion and the TBC1D3 family recover and plateau at 0.95 to 0.90; a rule that picks the pool per locus rather than genome-wide is not tested here.
- Rule 1 is a cheap, local repair of the representative, scoped to NPIP G98 and A; it is not free at the family level (bipartite F up to .022, pairwise up to .057 at NPIP G98) and its effect elsewhere is mixed (item 5). A native implementation (a `primary_reads` attribute, `mcl_families --representative primary-first`) is not justified by one dev library.

## Limits

One library per species; the human block is the development block of both families (nothing is held out for them). E-found is the strict, cap-based criterion on human reads only; M2 does not require the exact locus to be a node (M2n does). The copy truth is one annotation (CAT/Liftoff for human; RefSeq/Gnomon for gorilla); two human copies are not reachable (TBC1D3P7 has no exons; NPIPB13's truth has no transcripts yet it counts as expressed from reads alone and is never exact). The registered exons file puts PKD1P6-NPIPP1 on the NPIPP1 half (chr16:15,105,363-15,124,458) while the copy table and truth GTF carry the Liftoff gene about 2 kb away (15,126,202-15,159,411). Counts are of copies (25, 16, 24, 14) and a difference of one is one copy; the sweep is descriptive. Post hoc (added after seeing results, labelled where quoted): M6 for the human arms, red, CP*, M2n, the target-family rows, `secondary_support.py` and the G95+R1 reading; the red and CP* columns came after all arms had run. The instruments were registered by text and committed after the human arms (`bench/seed_pool/*` first in a27786a6); the human arms were produced by the driver of dae62624 (its single-contig path is identical in HEAD; the multi-contig branch came later) and an earlier `composition.py`, whose sha1 their stamps record; the gorilla widened arms were sharded (the shard wrapper is byte-identical to a single process on human chr17, gate G4, not re-checked on gorilla). Changing the driver changes the figures run-cache code hash of every stage although the default products are byte-identical (a one-off re-stamp is expected). Aligner intermediates were deleted after each arm (the PAF record counts are kept).

**What gorilla NPIP shows, and what it does not.** Under P, G995, G98 and G95 no locus carries any annotated intron of any of the 9 expressed copies (0, by the representative and by any transcript of the locus), while the all pool gives 2 to 4 annotated introns at 7 of the 9 (NPIPA5 and NPIPB14P stay at 0) and a node to 22 of 24 copies against 11. The registered S1 and S3 therefore fail in the direction of `all`. But: (i) of the 247 secondary alignments that carry at least 2 annotated introns at the 9 copies, 229 (93%) belong to molecules whose primary alignment lies inside another NPIP copy (1 in the same copy, 17 elsewhere; gorilla TBC1D3: 435 of 515, 84%) and only 0 to 5 per copy have an alignment score within 0.98 of the molecule's best, so the chains the all pool builds at a copy are largely paralog reads placed there, which is not evidence that the copy is expressed (verification scripts in `/mnt/linuxdisk/tmp/seed_pool_verify/`); (ii) the copies do have primary alignments (2 to 9, median 2, against 18 to 36 secondary, median 29; human NPIP 156 against 3,427, human TBC1D3 150 against 2,037, gorilla TBC1D3 6 against 77, all over the Amendment-A expressed sets of 24, 13, 9 and 7 copies; `bench/seed_pool/secondary_support.py`), but no chain is carried by more than 2 identical reads and 11 of the 12 copies that are not found have 1, below the assembler's per-chain floors; whether the pool or those floors is the cause is not separated by this design (the 09-29 trace attributed the gorilla NPIP absences to the pass-1 floor 3, the gate 8 and the polish mono floor 3); (iii) the contrast with human is not like for like: under Amendment A in human the numbers of copies found within nodes are NPIP P 15, G995 11, G98 11, G95 9, G90 8, A 9 of 24 and TBC1D3 P 10 of 13 (G98 12), i.e. also best at P, and the found fractions at P (63%, 57%, 0% for medians of 156, 6 and 2 primaries) show no dose-response between 156 and 6; (iv) the gorilla `all` step is a step at A, not a trend (M1 9, 11, 11, 11, 22 as in human).
