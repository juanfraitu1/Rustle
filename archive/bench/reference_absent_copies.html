Reference-absent copies · O4

Finding copies the reference doesn't show

A gene-family copy can be real and expressed yet invisible in the reference — collapsed onto its sibling, or present but unannotated. We recover both from the reads alone, then detect and flag, never fabricate: a flag becomes a copy only after assembly + genome homology + protein confirmation. Mode 1 · A collapsed copy, hiding in the pileup

One reference locus, its reads stacked below. Most columns are monomorphic — every read agrees, that's the copy the assembly kept. But a subset of columns carry a balanced second allele (20–60%) that appears on the same reads every time. Co-segregation is the tell: those reads are a second copy collapsed onto this locus. Reveal the columns and watch the hidden copy separate out.

Firewall — a genuine copy shows a diverse ref→alt spectrum; A→I editing is A→G / T→C-dominated, so those columns are dropped (this is what stops an edited base from faking a copy — it caught a GSTM false flag).
Honest funnel — the yield is small and disciplined. 357 reference-copy loci across 70 families scanned in 19 s → 30 flagged → 18 survive the editing firewall → 4 promote to confirmed reference-absent copies (assembly + genome homology + BLAST). All 4 are MHC genes clustered in the gorilla MHC region (NC_073229.2, 49–50 Mb) — the most polymorphic, copy-number-variable, reference-divergent region in the genome, exactly where you'd predict.

Mode 2 · A copy the annotation never named

Different question: not "is the sequence there" but "did anyone label it." We take every de-novo expressed locus and intersect it with the entire gorilla annotation (30,191 gene / mRNA / lncRNA / pseudogene spans). What overlaps nothing is expressed from a locus the annotation missed — and if that transcript also maps to ≥2 places, it's an unannotated family copy.

5×
The literally-unknown flagship. NC_073236.2 : 139.89 Mb — a spliced 183-aa expressed transcript at a locus with no gene, no lncRNA, no pseudogene, not even a LOC id, whose sequence maps to ~5–7 paralog loci at ≥80% identity. Sequence complexity 0.96 (not a repeat), no hit to any known gorilla protein. A candidate multi-copy gene family that was previously completely unknown.

Where the honesty line is. Collapsed detection is mechanism-strong but copy-confirmed only after assembly + BLAST — of hundreds of mechanism-level flags, 4 are locked. For the near-identical cases (a copy 0–3% divergent from its sibling), RNA alone cannot separate a collapsed copy from a heterozygous allele — that is precisely where DNA copy-number (parCN) is needed, and we say so rather than guess. The unannotated copies are real expressed loci absent from the annotation; the 4 "literally unknown" families are strong candidates pending cross-species protein confirmation.
