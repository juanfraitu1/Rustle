How to read this honestly

  • Human is testis Iso-Seq (A119b, downsampled 68M→4M to match the apes). Testis-expressed families (MAGEA, SRGAP2, FAM72, RBMY, NPIP, NBPF) are measured; a brain-only gene like ARHGAP11 is invisible here and stays inferred.
  • The measured human wins are real: SRGAP2 (2) and FAM72 (3) vs single-copy in all three apes — the human-specific expansion, confirmed from human's own reads, not the literature.
  • Multi-species branches are reliable (ancestral, African, human-chimp — a family had to link across species). Purely lineage-specific counts are upper bounds: sequence linking can miss divergent orthologs and split one ancestral family into two lineage-specific ones.
  • Counts are RNA-expressed copies, a proxy for genomic copy number; a DNA (WSSD/segdup) leg would turn them into famCN on the same tree.
