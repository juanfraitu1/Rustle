O1 · definition

Is it one family?

The three loci are mutually homologous → one family (a γ-quasi-clique). Homology decides membership; the reads do not vote on it. The read-conflict/PSV graph never defines the family — it counts copies.

O2 · assignment

Which copy made each read?

Each copy is a path; PSV columns are the bubbles where they differ. A read threads its observed alleles onto a copy-path and is assigned when the match is significant — else certified tied.
