Missing copies

KB3781 carries copies of some genes on one parental haplotype that the other lacks. Align the same Iso-Seq reads to each haplotype and the copy is missing from one genome and present in the other, which shows where the reads of a missing copy go and what a reference-free step can rebuild from them.

Can a reference-free step recover them?

IsoCon clusters the family's reads without the reference; the in-house stage builds candidates from the same reads. Outputs are linked back to the reference when they sit within the allele cutoff (0.00958) of a locus there; the rest are candidate new copies, and a candidate counts as flagged with at least two transcripts. Truth (the other genome) is used only to score. R1: every large copy beyond the cutoff is recovered. R2: no copy within the cutoff is flagged as new. R3: at most 20% of families without such a copy carry a false flag.

One animal (KB3781, male), fibroblast Iso-Seq only: the testis library is another animal. Every read in these sets was aligned to the combined primary assembly first, so "unmapped" here means mapped there and lost on this genome; reads unmapped on the primary assembly are counted separately above. Identities are asm20 against the reference genome with -N 50 -p 0.1. Few loci are expressed: this shows each copy, not a rate.
