How a read is assigned to a copy

A read that fits several copies (MAPQ 0) is assigned by evidence, not similarity. Reveal its bases one PSV at a time: every position that matches only one copy is copy-specific evidence, and the chance it arose by sequencing error, p ≈ εᵏ, plunges. Assign when p < α/(K−1); otherwise the read stays tied. Pick a read and press play.
