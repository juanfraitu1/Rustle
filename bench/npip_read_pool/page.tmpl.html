Which alignments should build the NPIP loci?

The proposal: build loci from every alignment, primaries and secondaries, and let the later all-vs-all minimap2 step remove the false positives. Below, the same pipeline run three ways on real data. Only the read pool that builds the loci changes.

Three read pools, one pipeline

Same frozen binary, same polish, same downstream steps: one locus per gene, a minimap2 all-vs-all of the locus spans (-x asm20 -c -X -N 50 -p 0.1), then mcl_families. Only the alignments that may build a locus change.

What the step does and does not remove

    What this means

    • The concern is right, the remedy is not. At NPIP most primaries are coin tosses, so primaries alone can put a locus at the wrong copy. Adding every secondary does not decide where a tied read came from. It copies the read to every copy it ties with, and the family step cannot undo that.
    • Which copy a tied read came from is decided after the loci exist, by the positions where copies differ (PSVs): assign the read or abstain, never split it 1/k. That step works the same whichever pool built the loci.
    • Locus existence is the part the pool decides. Primaries only: 23 of 25 copies with their own node, 11 extra nodes. All alignments: 24 copies (NPIPB2 is the gain) for 87 extra nodes and 22× the all-vs-all work. Good secondaries: in between on extras, but two copies fewer on NPIP. Its genome-scale held-out gain (gorilla, 23 Sep) is not overturned by one family.
    • The two copies with no untied primary (LOC124907807, LOC124907808) are the case for an existence-only call: present, but never assigned reads (--tied-seed).
