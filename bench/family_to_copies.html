From a family to its copies

The bridge between the two graphs: a family (the nodes) becomes a set of copies, each with a reference sequence; the PSVs a read is later assigned by are the columns where they differ — genotyped across the reads (a pileup), or, for a collapsed copy, called directly from the reads. Crucially, the reference is the assembly — not a consensus built from the reads.

reads → loci → families → copies → PSVs → assign
