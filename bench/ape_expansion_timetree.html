How to read this honestly

  • Counts are RNA-expressed multi-copy loci, matched to ~4M reads across apes — a proxy for genomic copy number, not a DNA copy-number call.
  • The multi-species branches are reliable (ancestral, African-ape) — they required a family to link across species. Purely lineage-specific counts are upper bounds: sequence linking can miss divergent orthologs and split one ancestral family into two "lineage-specific" ones.
  • NPIP/NBPF/POTE magnitudes come from detection, not annotation — most paralogs are unnamed (LOC…) in these assemblies, so gene-name counts undercount them. NPIP's gorilla copy (3) is the sequence-linked value; NBPF/POTE gorilla detection is uncertain.
  • The human-lineage tier is the strongest claim: SRGAP2, ARHGAP11, FAM72 are single-copy in all three apes we sampled — direct evidence their expansion is human-specific. TBC1D3 is annotated but barely RNA-detected in apes.
